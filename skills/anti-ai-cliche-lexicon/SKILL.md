# Anti-AI Cliché Lexicon & Transition De-Sterilization

This skill catalogues banned AI vocabulary, mechanical transitions, and synthetic rhetorical formulas, providing natural, idiomatic human alternatives.

---

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Banned Buzzwords

These words and phrases are statistical dead-giveaways of synthetic LLM text. **Zero occurrences permitted in final rewrites:**

| Banned LLM Term | Why It Fails | Natural Human Replacement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Delve" / "Delve into"** | The quintessential LLM verb; rarely used in modern speech | Explore, inspect, dig into, examine, unpack, look at |
| **"Tapestry"** | Pretentious metaphor for complex systems or histories | Mixture, web, reality, fabric, history, landscape |
| **"Beacon"** | Overused sentimental imagery (*beacon of hope/innovation*) | Guide, leader, benchmark, standout example |
| **"Testament to"** | Hyperbolic signposting (*stands as a testament*) | Proof of, shows that, demonstrates, reflects |
| **"Plethora" / "Myriad"** | Archaic, sterile quantification (*a plethora of options*) | Plenty of, dozens of, countless, a wide range of |
| **"Foster"** | Bureaucratic filler (*foster collaboration/growth*) | Build, encourage, spark, nurture, drive, support |
| **"Pivotal" / "Crucial" / "Paramount"** | Over-dramatized importance padding | Key, decisive, essential, critical, central |
| **"Navigating the complexities"** | Generic corporate buzz-phrase | Handling, tackling, working through, sorting out |
| **"At its core" / "In essence"** | Redundant throat-clearing | Basically, fundamentally, at heart, simply put |
| **"Shed light on"** | Formulaic epistemic idiom | Clarify, explain, reveal, expose |
| **"Intertwined" / "Interconnected"** | Reflexive AI pairing (*deeply intertwined*) | Linked, tied together, tangled, related |
| **"Serves as a reminder"** | Preachy, moralizing rhetorical posture | Reminds us, warns, highlights, illustrates |

## 2. Mechanical Transitions vs. Organic Connectors

LLMs rely heavily on rigid sentence-level transitions placed formulaically at the start of paragraphs:

| Robotic Transition | Natural Alternative |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Furthermore" / "Moreover" / "Additionally"** | "Also", "On top of that", "Beyond that", or simply integrate into the sentence |
| **"In conclusion" / "To conclude"** | Cut entirely, or use "In the end", "Looking back", "Ultimately" |
| **"It is important to note that..."** | Strike the phrase and state the point directly |
| **"On the one hand... on the other hand..."** | "While X does Y, Z behaves differently" |
| **"Consequently" / "As a result"** | "So", "Because of this", "This meant", "That forced" |
| **"Notwithstanding the above"** | "Even so", "Still", "Despite that" |

## 3. Structural Tells to Eliminate

1. **Throat-Clearing Openers:**
   - ❌ *"When it comes to modern software development, efficiency is paramount."*
   - ✔️ *"Software teams move fast or die."*
2. **Moralizing or Preachy Conclusions:**
   - ❌ *"As we move forward into an uncertain future, only time will tell how this tapestry unfolds."*
   - ✔️ *"Expect more changes before year's end."*
3. **Symmetrical Both-Sides Hedging:**
   - ❌ *"While there are advantages to this approach, it is crucial to recognize that challenges remain."*
   - ✔️ *"It solves latency, but memory usage doubles."*
4. **Sterile Passive Voice:**
   - ❌ *"A decision was made by the engineering committee to terminate the project."*
   - ✔️ *"The engineering committee killed the project."*
